# PokemonChampions-APManual
Manual implementation for the Pokemon Champions game, for Archipelago Randomizer. Win 10 battles to Goal.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pokémon Champions is a free-to-start, cross-platform competitive battling game developed by The Pokémon Works for the Nintendo Switch, iOS, and Android devices. Focusing entirely on high-stakes player-versus-player (PvP) combat utilizing the classic turn-based mechanics, precise type advantages, and abilities.

# How to play
(Of course you need Manual from Archipelago, and a copy of Pokemon Champions. Is free!)
To play this manual, you can start by creating any team you desire, with any pokemon at your possession. The system this games has it's already pretty randomized anyways.

However, there'll be restrictions, at the start of the game:
- ** You're not going to be able to use** **your __Pokemon's Move slots #2,#3 and #4.__**
- ** You're not going to be able to use** **__Mega Evolution__**
- and **You're not going to be able to use** **__equipable Items__**

These will all be obtained through items on the multiworld.

# Checks
You basically collect 2 checks per combat win:
- First you collect your X Round pass, allowing you to collect your X victory check and pass to the next round.
- Second, you collect your X victory. This is a randomised check.

# Items
- +1 move slots

# Goal
Your goal is as straightforward as to __win 10 fights.__ 
Doesn't matter if you lose midway there, just keep battling until you've obtained 10 victories.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Notes / Misc.
- Deathlink can be enabled. If you receive one, you shall run off from your current fight.
- If you're ever stuck, you shall run from a fight. (For example, you only have 1 PKMN left, and it's 1 unique enabled move is out of PP/is blocked by the rival's move. In this case you shall use the only other button you can: Exit.)
- I made this with 1v1 modality in mind, but, I gueeeessss if you're really luckyyyyy, you could make rounds on 2v2 as well, but know I have not tested it on this modality
- Both blocked and unlocking movements slots affect to your entire team all at once.
